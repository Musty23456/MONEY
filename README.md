# Manajan Kudi

Android app (Kotlin + WebView) wanda ke nuna "Manajan Kudi" — app na sarrafa kudi
da aka rubuta da HTML/CSS/JavaScript, an naɗe shi cikin native Android shell.
Ana adana bayanai a na'urar (localStorage) - babu bukatar intanet don amfani da shi
bayan an shigar da app ɗin.

## Gina APK
Push zuwa `main`, ko a tafiyar da workflow da hannu daga Actions tab.
Za a sami APK a matsayin artifact mai suna `manajan-kudi-debug-apk`.
